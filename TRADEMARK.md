# StepAway name and logo policy

The **source code in this repository** is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later (see `LICENSE`):
anyone may use it, study it, change it and contribute to it. The GPL also lets others
redistribute it under the same license.

The **name "StepAway"**, the app icon / logo and the app identifier
`io.github.sandipbera35.StepAway` are **not** licensed under the GPL (as GPL section 7(e)
allows). They identify the official project, and they may only be used as described here.

## Always fine
- Saying what the software is: "works with StepAway", "a fork of StepAway", "based on StepAway".
- Linking to the official project and downloads.
- Unmodified copies of the **official releases** (same binaries and checksums as on the
  GitHub Releases page), with the name unchanged, passed on together with the license.
- Using the name for your own copy that you only run, or for contributions you send back.

## Please do not
- Publish a **modified** version, or a build of modified source, under the name "StepAway", with
  the StepAway logo, or with the same app identifier. Choose your own name, icon and
  identifier, and say clearly that it is a fork.
- Suggest that a fork, store listing or website is the official StepAway or is endorsed by the
  project.
- Publish in app stores (Google Play, Microsoft Store, Flathub, F-Droid, Snap Store, ...) an
  app using the StepAway name or logo unless it is the official build published by the
  maintainer.

## Why
People trust the name to mean "the app that does what the privacy policy says". A modified copy
under the same name could behave differently (for example collect data or weaken the lock) while
looking official. Keeping the name for the official build protects users.

## Not legal advice / limits
This policy cannot take away rights the GPL gives you over the code, and it cannot stop anyone
from redistributing a renamed copy under the GPL. It applies to the name, the logo and the app
identifier only. Releases published before this policy (v0.1.0 to v0.1.2) were published under
the GPL alone; this policy applies to the name from now on.
